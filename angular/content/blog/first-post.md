Welcome! I’m Everton, a front-end developer based in Setúbal, Portugal. I’ve been building for the web since 2005, and this is a place to share some of what I’ve learned along the way.

I enjoy turning complex problems into clear, useful experiences. That can mean figuring out how to structure an interface, making a design work well across devices, or finding a simpler way to build and maintain a feature.

Here, I’ll write about the practical side of that work: things I’ve learned, approaches I’ve tried, and ideas worth exploring further. The goal isn’t to have the last word, but to share notes that might help someone else working through a similar problem.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something useful.
